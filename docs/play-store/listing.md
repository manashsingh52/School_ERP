**Full description** (4,000 characters max)

```
Bastaa keeps parents and teachers in step with the school, in one simple app.

This is a demo with a sample school. The names and numbers are made up, and nothing you change is saved. Tap "Explore as a parent" or "Explore as a teacher" to look around.

FOR PARENTS
• See today at a glance: is your child in school, today's classes, homework due and fees
• Attendance calendar for every month, and the whole session
• Homework from every subject in one place
• Timetable for the week
• Exam results with a subject-by-subject chart and progress over time
• Fees: what's due, what's overdue, and receipts for everything paid
• Message your child's class teacher and subject teachers, or the school office
• Video meetings with teachers, reminders and the school calendar

FOR TEACHERS
• Take attendance in seconds, even without a connection
• Enter exam marks on your phone
• Post homework with photos from your camera
• Messages and meetings with parents
• Your classes for the day and the school calendar

One app for parents and teachers. If you're both, switch between them in Profile.
```
