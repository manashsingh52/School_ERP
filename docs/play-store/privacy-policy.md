# Bastaa demo app: privacy policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

This policy covers the **Bastaa demo app** on Google Play (package `com.manashsingh.schoolerp`).

## What the demo is

The demo shows how Bastaa works for parents and teachers. It uses a made-up sample school:
every student, teacher, parent, mark, fee and message in it is invented. Nobody signs in with a
real account.

## Data we collect

**None.** The demo doesn't collect, store or share any personal information:

- There is no account and no sign-in with real details. The "Explore as a parent" and "Explore as a
  teacher" buttons open the sample school.
- The sample data is part of the app. The app doesn't send anything to us or to anyone else.
- Anything you change (attendance, marks, messages, reminders, a test payment) stays on your phone
  and is gone when the app closes.
- A photo you pick for a homework post stays on your phone and isn't uploaded.
- Payments in the demo are simulated. No money moves and no card or bank details are asked for.
- There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Links that leave the app

A "Join meeting" link opens Jitsi Meet (meet.jit.si) in your browser. That's a separate service
with its own privacy policy.

## Children

The app is for parents and school staff, not for children.

## The full Bastaa service

When schools use the full Bastaa service, a separate privacy policy will explain what school data
is kept, why, and for how long. This policy will be updated before the app connects to a real
school.
